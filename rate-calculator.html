💡 Rate-Setting Tips

  • Start higher than you think: Clients respect higher rates. You can always negotiate down, but never up.
  • Different rates for different clients: Charge premium clients more. Offer discounts for long-term retainers.
  • Review every 6 months: Increase rates 10-20% annually to keep pace with inflation and your growing skills.
  • Project pricing beats hourly: Once you're experienced, switch to fixed-price projects for better margins.
  • Value > time: Charge based on the value you deliver, not just hours worked. A 2-hour fix that saves R100k is worth more than R400.
