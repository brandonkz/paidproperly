💰 Tax Deductions You Can Claim Save R65k+/year

As a freelancer, these business expenses reduce your taxable income before SARS calculates your tax. Track everything with receipts.

🏠 Home Office (~R3,000-5,000/month)

  • Rent/Bond: Claim % of total based on office space (e.g., 10% of R15k rent = R1,500/month)
  • Electricity: Same % as rent (10% of R1,500 = R150/month)
  • Water & rates: Same % (10% of R800 = R80/month)
  • Internet: 100% if only used for work, or proportional split (R700-1,200/month)

💻 Equipment & Tech (Full cost deductible)

  • Laptop/Computer: R15,000-40,000 (depreciate over 3 years or claim immediately if
  • Monitor, keyboard, mouse: R3,000-8,000
  • Software subscriptions: Adobe CC (R680/month), Office 365 (R99/month), Zoom (R150/month)
  • Cloud storage: Google Drive, Dropbox (R100-300/month)
  • Backup drives: R1,500-3,000

📊 Professional Services (R2,000-10,000/year)

  • Accountant fees: R2,000-5,000/year for tax filing
  • Legal fees: Contract reviews, business setup
  • Professional memberships: Industry associations, certifications
  • Bank fees: Business account charges, international transfer fees

📱 Cellphone & Data (R500-1,500/month)

  • Contract: Claim business % (e.g., 80% of R800 = R640/month)
  • Data bundles: Vodacom, MTN, Cell C business data
  • Backup phone: For work calls only

🚗 Work-Related Travel (Keep logbook)

  • Client meetings: Uber/Bolt fares, parking
  • Business travel: Flights, accommodation for client work
  • Car expenses: Fuel, maintenance (if you use car for work - claim per km at R4.29/km for 2026)

🎯 Retirement Annuity (RA) HUGE TAX SAVER

Claim up to 27.5% of your income, capped at R350,000/year. This is the single biggest tax deduction for high earners.

Example:

  • Annual income: R1,110,000 ($5k/month × R18.50 × 12)
  • RA contribution: R3,000/month × 12 = R36,000/year
  • Taxable income: R1,110,000 - R36,000 = R1,074,000
  • Tax saved: ~R14,400/year (at 40% marginal rate)

🏥 Medical Aid Tax Credit (R364/month per person)

Not a deduction, but a tax credit that reduces your tax directly. R364/month for main member + first dependent, R246/month for each additional dependent (2026 rates).

📝 Pro Tips:

  • Keep ALL receipts: SARS can audit you up to 5 years later. Scan and upload to Google Drive monthly.
  • Track expenses in real-time: Use Xero, QuickBooks, or even a Google Sheet. Don't wait until tax season.
  • Home office %: Measure your office space vs total home size. Be conservative (SARS checks this).
  • Separate bank account: Business transactions only. Makes tracking 10x easier.
  • Get an accountant if you earn >R500k/year: They'll find deductions you missed. Costs R2-5k/year, saves R20-50k.
