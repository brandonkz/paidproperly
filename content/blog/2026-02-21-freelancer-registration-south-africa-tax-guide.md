# How to Register as a Freelancer in South Africa: Complete 2026 Tax Guide

**Last updated:** February 21, 2026

So you've landed your first remote USD gig. Congratulations 🎉

But before you start spending that money, you need to sort out your tax situation. This guide covers **everything you need to register as a freelancer in South Africa** — tax numbers, VAT, provisional tax, and deductions.

## TL;DR (Too Long; Didn't Read)

**Must-do immediately:**
1. Register for a **tax number** with SARS (if you don't have one)
2. Register as a **provisional taxpayer** (deadline: within 1 month of earning income)
3. Register for **VAT** if you earn over R1 million/year (mandatory threshold)
4. Track ALL expenses (they're tax-deductible)
5. Pay provisional tax **twice a year** (Aug 31 & Feb 28)

**Penalties for not doing this:** Interest, fines, and potential criminal charges. SARS doesn't play.

---

## Step 1: Get a Tax Number (Income Tax Reference Number)

### If you already have a tax number:
Skip to Step 2.

### If you don't have one:
1. Go to [SARS eFiling](https://www.sarsefiling.co.za)
2. Click "Register" → "Individual"
3. You'll need:
   - ID number
   - Proof of address (bank statement, utility bill <3 months old)
   - Cellphone (for SMS verification)
   - Email address
4. SARS will issue your **Income Tax Reference Number** within 24-48 hours

**Cost:** Free  
**Time:** 30 minutes to register, 1-2 days for approval

---

## Step 2: Register as a Provisional Taxpayer

This is the **most important step** for freelancers. 

### What is provisional tax?
As a freelancer, your clients don't deduct tax from your payments (unlike a salary). So SARS requires you to **pay tax twice a year** based on your estimated annual income.

### Deadlines:
- **First payment:** August 31 (pays 50% of estimated tax)
- **Second payment:** February 28 (pays remaining 50%)

### How to register:
1. Log into SARS eFiling
2. Go to **SARS Registered Details** → **Register for Tax Type**
3. Select **Income Tax: Individual**
4. Tick **Provisional Tax** checkbox
5. Submit

**Deadline:** Within **21 business days** of receiving your first freelance payment  
**Cost:** Free  
**Penalty for late registration:** R250/month

---

## Step 3: Do You Need to Register for VAT?

### Mandatory VAT registration:
If your **annual taxable income exceeds R1 million**, you MUST register for VAT within 21 days.

### Voluntary VAT registration:
If you earn between R50,000 - R1 million/year, you CAN register for VAT (optional). Benefits:
- Claim back VAT on business expenses (internet, laptop, software)
- Looks more professional to corporate clients

**Downside:** 15% admin burden (VAT returns every 2 months)

### How to register:
1. SARS eFiling → **Register for Tax Type**
2. Select **Value-Added Tax**
3. Choose registration date (must be within 21 days of hitting threshold)
4. SARS will issue a **VAT number** within 21 days

## Step 4: Track Your Expenses (Tax Deductions for Freelancers)

One of the **biggest advantages of freelancing** is that you can deduct business expenses from your income before paying tax.

### What you can claim:
✅ **Home office** (percentage of rent/bond, electricity, internet)  
✅ **Computer & tech** (laptop, monitor, keyboard, software subscriptions)  
✅ **Cellphone** (business portion of contract)  
✅ **Internet & data** (100% if used only for work)  
✅ **Professional fees** (accountant, lawyer, business courses)  
✅ **Travel** (if you travel for work — Uber to client meetings, flights)  
✅ **Retirement annuity** (up to 27.5% of income, capped at R350,000/year)

### What you CANNOT claim:
❌ Personal groceries  
❌ Gym membership (unless you're a fitness content creator)  
❌ Entertainment (Netflix, DSTV)  
❌ Clothing (unless it's work uniform/branded gear)

### How to track:
Use a simple spreadsheet or app like **Xero, QuickBooks, or Wave** (Wave is free).

**Example:**
```
Income: $5,000/month × R18.50 = R92,500/month = R1.11M/year
Deductions: R15,000/month (home office, internet, laptop, retirement)
Taxable income: R1.11M - R180k = R930k
Tax: ~R145k/year (vs R210k without deductions)
Savings: R65,000/year
```

## Step 5: Pay Provisional Tax (Twice a Year)

### How much to pay?
SARS uses this formula:

**First payment (Aug 31):** 50% of your estimated annual tax  
**Second payment (Feb 28):** Remaining 50% + adjustments

### How to calculate:
1. Estimate your annual income (USD × 12 × exchange rate)
2. Subtract deductions (home office, retirement, etc.)
3. Use [SARS tax tables](https://www.sars.gov.za/tax-rates/income-tax/rates-of-tax-for-individuals/) to calculate tax
4. Divide by 2

**Or use our calculator:** [USD Salary Calculator](https://paidproperly.co.za/usd-salary-calculator.html) — it shows your provisional tax amount.

### How to pay:
1. SARS eFiling → **Returns** → **Provisional Tax**
2. Complete **IRP6** return
3. SARS generates a payment reference
4. Pay via EFT, eFiling, or any bank

**Penalty for late/short payment:** 10% underestimation penalty + interest

## Step 6: File Your Annual Tax Return

Even though you pay provisional tax, you MUST still file an **ITR12** return by **October 23** every year.

This is where SARS:
- Checks if you paid enough provisional tax
- Refunds you if you overpaid
- Charges you if you underpaid

### What you need:
- **IRP5** (if you had a salary before freelancing)
- **All invoices** from clients
- **Proof of expenses** (receipts, bank statements)
- **Retirement annuity certificate** (RA fund issues this)
- **Medical aid certificate**

### How to file:
1. SARS eFiling → **Returns** → **Income Tax**
2. Complete **ITR12** form
3. Upload supporting documents
4. Submit

**Deadline:** October 23, 2026 (for 2025/2026 tax year)  
**Penalty for late filing:** R250/month

## Common Mistakes Freelancers Make

### ❌ **"I'll register when I earn more money"**
SARS requires registration **within 21 days** of first income. Penalties accrue from day 1.

### ❌ **"I can ignore provisional tax and just pay at year-end"**
SARS charges 10% underestimation penalty + interest if you don't pay provisionally.

### ❌ **"My client pays me USD, so SARS won't know"**
Foreign income is **fully taxable** in SA if you're a tax resident. Banks report international transfers to SARS automatically.

### ❌ **"I don't need receipts, I'll estimate my expenses"**
SARS can audit you up to **5 years** after filing. No receipts = no deductions = big tax bill.

## FAQs

### Do I need to register a company to freelance?
**No.** You can operate as a **sole proprietor** (individual) using just your ID number. Only register a company (Pty Ltd) if:
- You earn over R2M/year (tax benefits)
- You want limited liability protection
- Clients require it

### Can I use my personal tax number for freelancing?
**Yes.** Your personal tax number works for freelance income. No need for a separate business tax number unless you register a company.

### What if I miss a provisional tax payment?
SARS charges:
- **10% underestimation penalty** (if you pay <80% of actual tax)
- **Interest** on unpaid amounts (~10%/year)

Pay as soon as you realize you missed it.

### Can I claim home office if I rent?
**Yes.** You can claim a percentage of your rent based on the portion of your home used exclusively for work. Example: If your home office is 10% of your home's floor space, claim 10% of rent + utilities.

### Do I pay tax on crypto/forex gains?
**Yes.** SARS treats crypto and forex trading as taxable income. Capital gains tax applies if you hold for investment, income tax if you trade frequently.
