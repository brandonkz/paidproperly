How Two-Pot Retirement Works

Savings Pot (10%): You can withdraw from this pot once a year (minimum R2,000). Tax is deducted according to your marginal tax rate.

Retirement Pot (90%): Locked until retirement — cannot be withdrawn early.

Important: Withdrawals are taxed as income. This calculator estimates tax based on typical marginal rates, but your exact tax depends on your total income.
