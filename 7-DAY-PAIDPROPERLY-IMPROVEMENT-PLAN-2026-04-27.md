# PaidProperly 7-Day Improvement Plan

_Date: 2026-04-27_

## Goal
Increase affiliate clicks now, while building better future monetization through owned audience + better recirculation.

## Day 1 — Fix conversion paths and analytics
- Add a **Start Here** triage section on the homepage so visitors pick the right lane faster.
- Add an inline **newsletter shortlist CTA** instead of relying only on the modal.
- Track actual **affiliate redirect page loads** so we can measure which platforms generate clicks.
- **Status:** Implemented.

## Day 2 — Improve recirculation on high-intent pages
- Add **related platforms** to platform detail pages.
- Keep users on-site longer when their first option isn’t the fit.
- **Status:** Implemented.

## Day 3 — Tighten the best-money pages
- Rewrite the homepage hero and “Best Chance” copy based on intent buckets:
  - quick side income
  - remote salary replacement
  - skilled freelancer/high-pay
- Add clearer language around payout speed, likelihood, and skill barrier.

## Day 4 — Build an owned lead magnet
- Create a simple lead magnet:
  - “7 SA-friendly platforms to apply to this week”
  - or “Remote work starter pack for South Africans”
- Hook the Beehiiv signup to this offer instead of a generic newsletter pitch.

## Day 5 — Monetization pass
- Review every top platform in `data/platforms.json` for affiliate/referral status.
- Prioritize high-intent/high-trust entries first:
  - Respondent
  - Somewhere
  - Remote Rocketship
  - Toptal
  - Preply / Italki / Turing if viable
- Mark pages where we still have plain outbound links so they’re easy to upgrade later.

## Day 6 — SEO + money pages
- Publish or refresh pages that can rank and monetize:
  - best entry-level remote work for South Africans
  - best sites that actually pay South Africans
  - fastest-paying side hustles in South Africa
  - remote salary vs freelance income calculator comparison
- Add stronger internal links from blog posts into `/go/` pages and calculators.

## Day 7 — Review and double down
- Check which categories get the most clicks.
- Promote the top-converting bucket harder on the homepage.
- If one CTA clearly wins, simplify the page around it instead of spreading attention too thin.

## What shipped immediately
1. Homepage **Start Here** section with goal-based paths.
2. Homepage **inline shortlist CTA** for email capture.
3. Platform detail page **related options** section.
4. Redirect-page analytics for **real affiliate click tracking**.
