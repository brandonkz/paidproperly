Frequently Asked Questions

What is PaidProperly?

PaidProperly is a curated directory of legitimate platforms that accept and pay South African remote workers. We vet every platform to ensure they actually pay out and don't ghost South Africans at withdrawal time.

Do these platforms really pay South Africans?

Yes! Every platform listed here has been verified to accept South African users and process payments. We exclude platforms with poor payout histories or those that restrict access from South Africa.

What types of remote work are available?

Our directory includes: Tech & Development (freelance coding, software development), Customer Support, Testing & Research (user testing, surveys), Teaching & Mentoring, Writing & Transcription, AI Training, Freelance Marketplaces, and Remote Job Boards.

How do I get paid?

Payment methods vary by platform but most support: PayPal, Payoneer, Direct Bank Transfer (EFT), Wise (formerly TransferWise), and some support cryptocurrency. Check each platform's payout details before signing up.

Are there entry-level opportunities?

Yes! We tag platforms by difficulty level. Entry-level (Easy) platforms like UserTesting, Respondent, and Appen don't require advanced skills or experience. Filter by "Entry Level" to see beginner-friendly options.

Is this free to use?

Yes, PaidProperly is completely free. We may earn commissions when you sign up through our links, which helps us keep the site running and up-to-date. This doesn't cost you anything extra.
