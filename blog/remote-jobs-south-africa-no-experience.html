Remote Jobs in South Africa With No Experience: 20+ Platforms That Pay

October 27, 2026 5 min read

You don't need a degree or five years of experience to start earning online from South Africa. You need a laptop, stable internet, a way to get paid in dollars, and the patience to pass a test or two.

The hard part isn't finding "remote jobs". Google is full of them. The hard part is finding the ones that accept South Africans, take beginners and actually pay out. That's what this list is for.

Every platform below is tagged beginner-friendly and SA-friendly on PaidProperly. Pay figures are the typical ranges each platform publishes or that workers report. Your results will depend on your hours, your skills and how much work is available.

Before you sign up anywhere: 4 checks

Run these on any site, including the ones on this list.

  1. Does it pay to South Africa? Look for PayPal, Payoneer or Wise. If the only option is a US bank account, move on.
  2. What's the minimum payout? Some sites hold your money until you reach $10, $50 or more.
  3. Does it charge you to join? Real platforms pay you. If a "job" asks for a registration fee or a starter kit, it's a scam.
  4. Have South Africans been paid? Search the platform name plus "South Africa" on Reddit or MyBroadband.

More on spotting fakes in our remote work scams guide.

1. Website and app testing

Testing is the easiest starting point. You use a website or app, say what you think out loud, and get paid per test.

  • UserTesting: $4 to $10 per 20-minute test, paid weekly via PayPal. Short tests, but invites can be slow at first.
  • Respondent: $50 to $400 per research study. You need to match the profile a company is looking for, so you won't qualify for most studies. The ones you do get pay well.
  • Testbirds: €20 to €50 per test, paid by PayPal or bank transfer. Includes bug hunting, which rewards attention to detail.

Best for: students, side income, anyone with a quiet room and a decent mic.

2. AI training and data work

AI companies need people to label data, rate answers and check translations. Work comes in projects, so it can be busy one month and quiet the next.

  • Scale AI: $10 to $25 an hour, with regular payments.
  • OneForma: $5 to $25 an hour depending on the project. Pays via PayPal and Payoneer, usually twice a month. Good if you speak an African language.
  • Clickworker: $3 to $15 an hour for microtasks. Weekly payouts via PayPal or Payoneer.

Best for: detail-oriented people, multilingual South Africans.

3. Transcription

Listen to audio, type what you hear. Most sites make you pass a typing and grammar test first.

  • Rev: $0.30 to $1.10 per audio minute, weekly PayPal.
  • GoTranscript: up to $0.60 per audio minute. Weekly payouts via PayPal, Payoneer or USDC.
  • TranscribeMe: $15 to $22 per audio hour after you pass the exam. PayPal only.

Best for: fast, accurate typists. Expect low hourly earnings until your speed improves.

4. Online tutoring

South African English is a real advantage here. Students in Asia, Europe and Latin America pay for conversation practice.

  • Cambly: $10.20 an hour for adult tutoring, weekly PayPal. No teaching degree needed.
  • Preply: $15 to $25 an hour average. You set your own rates.
  • AmazingTalker: $10 to $30 an hour typical. Withdraw via Wise, PayPal or Payoneer.
  • Superprof South Africa: set your own rand rates for local students.

Best for: clear speakers, people who enjoy chatting, anyone with a subject they know well.

5. Customer support

This is the closest thing to a normal job. Many roles are full-time with training and benefits, but you'll need reliable internet and backup power.

  • TTEC: hourly pay plus benefits, with training provided.
  • Concentrix, Teleperformance and Foundever: large employers with remote roles. Pay varies by role and region.
  • Working Solutions: contractor roles paid per minute or per call, with a flexible schedule.

Best for: people who want steady hours instead of gig work.

6. Virtual assistant work

Admin, inbox management, scheduling and basic customer service for overseas businesses.

  • Time Etc: $11 to $16 an hour starting, rising with experience.
  • Cherry Assistant: $4 to $12 an hour typical. Focused on South African assistants.
  • 20four7VA and Wing Assistant: $4 to $12 an hour typical, matched with clients.

Best for: organised people with good written English.

Getting paid properly

Most of these platforms pay in dollars through PayPal or Payoneer. Fees and exchange rates eat into what lands in your account, so compare them before you choose. Our guides on getting paid as a South African freelancer and PayPal alternatives cover the options.

Online income is still taxable in South Africa. Keep a record of what you earn and set some aside for SARS. This is general information, not tax advice. Use our net salary calculator to see what you'll take home.

Bottom line

You can start earning online in South Africa with no experience. Start with testing or microtasks for quick wins. Move to tutoring, support or VA work for steadier income. Check every platform pays to SA before you put in the hours.
